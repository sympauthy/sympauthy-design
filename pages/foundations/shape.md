# Shape

How a surface is set apart from what is behind it, and what its corners say.

## Usage

- **The system is flat. Nothing casts a shadow**: not a card, a menu, a popover or a dialog. A surface is set apart by its `ink-200` border, and an open dialog also by its scrim.
- **A radius says what the thing is.** Each token below names what takes it, and nothing else does.
- **There is one card.** Its look, and what is built on it, is on [Card](/components/card).
